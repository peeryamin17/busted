## 1. Right now: there is nothing to refund

BugSeek AI does not currently take payments. No plans can be bought, no cards are charged, and no payment details are collected or stored. Until paid plans open, this page exists so the promise is written down before the first charge ever happens.

## 2. When paid plans open

When payments begin, this policy will state, in plain language and before any price is shown anywhere:

- what each plan costs, what a credit buys, and when credits expire;
- a refund window for first purchases (target: 14 days, unused or barely used plans);
- that unused credits are not cash and cannot be transferred between accounts;
- how to ask for a refund (one email, no forms, no retention scripts), and how quickly refunds are processed;
- that patrols and scans already run are delivered work and are counted fairly against usage before any refund is calculated.

Nothing in this draft is a pricing commitment. Prices, credit costs and limits will be published on the pricing page at launch and will not change retroactively for an active billing period.
